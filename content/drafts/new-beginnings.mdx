# The rush

Theres infinite discourse about not rushing life, but not enough people talk about how fast life ossifies.

24-27 is the first and last time in life where people have complete control over their lives. before that, your thoughts and actions are shaped by your parents, friends out of proximity, school, lack of resources.

At 24, you're out of school, the cusp of moving away from home, enough financial resources, and start to realize life is not as it seems.

After 27, your life and mind will start to be dictated by the thought ofseriously settling down with a partner. Perhaps it's later for other people, but I know for a fact that that's when I'm going to become more serious about real long term partnership in life. And by then, my actions will be dictated by my family.

This means by 24 to 27 is the core area of focusing on yourself. Your life should be dictated by purely understanding who you are, extinguishing all the random innocuous desires that you've wanted to live.

The side part is most people are limited by the realities of life. Maybe some people don't have infinite PTO and so they have to go into office four times a week. They don't have the money or luxury to travel to South America for a couple months and experience what that life is like without having an existential crisis about their career.

The sad part is because they're not able to make these and Jurassic choices and commit to these explorations, they miss out on perspective about how different people live their lives that will be important for their own growth.
