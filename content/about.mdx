I take ideas from 0 to 1.

I am currently on a world tour, exploring how the various cultures of the world operate, and exploring how my own mind operates in new uncomfortable situations.

I was previously a software engineer at Solana Labs. I built a payments product that processed over $80M in volume through integrations with platforms like Shopify, and developed production-grade backend infrastructure for on-chain game studios.

After graduating, I spent a year at home working and spending time with family, then moved to Fort Lauderdale in 2024—where a mix of travel, autonomy, and new friendships pushed me to rethink what I actually value.
